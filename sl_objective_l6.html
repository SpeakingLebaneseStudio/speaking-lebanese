Lesson Objective
Daily Routine & Habits

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to describe your daily routine from the moment you wake up to the moment you go to sleep — using the single biggest grammar milestone in this course: present-tense verb conjugation. You'll also be able to say what you're not doing, and describe how you're feeling with Lebanon's rich vocabulary for moods and emotions.

Bfee2, bektob, w berté7. Three verbs, three habits — that's a whole day in Lebanese.
