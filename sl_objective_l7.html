Lesson Objective
Getting Around & Shopping

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to find your way around — asking for and giving directions, and shop with confidence — asking prices, comparing items, and handling a small emergency if one comes up. This lesson carries the heaviest grammar load in the whole course: prepositions of place, imperatives (commands like "turn" and "stop here"), plurals, and comparatives — four building blocks working together in one set of real, everyday situations.
